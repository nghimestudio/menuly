=== Full description ===
Menuly is your AI waiter at the table.

Snap the menu. Choose a mood. Get one clear dish pick — with waiter-style reasoning, not a calorie spreadsheet.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Pick a mood: Recommend me, Budget, Delicious, High protein, or Green diet
2. Scan the restaurant menu with your camera
3. Menuly reads the menu and recommends the best match for how you feel right now
4. Ask Waiter follow-up questions if you want something different

WHY MENULY
• Mood-first recommendations — not endless ranked lists
• One decisive pick + a runner-up and what to skip
• Works across languages (English, Vietnamese, Chinese, Japanese, Korean)
• Keeps a local history of past suggestions
• Dark, fast UI designed for noisy restaurant lighting

BUILT FOR REAL DINING
Tourists decoding foreign menus. Groups that can’t decide. Anyone who wants a sharp suggestion without overthinking.

NOTES
• Recommendations are AI estimates based on scanned menu text — always double-check allergens and ingredients with staff.
• Camera access is used only to read the menu.
• Menu text is sent to our secure API so the AI can recommend a dish.

Menuly — sit down, scan, decide.
